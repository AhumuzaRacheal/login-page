How to Apply Online

Follow these quick steps to submit your application to MUST.

1

Create an Account

Click "+ Create Account" and fill in your Surname, Other Names, Phone Number, and Email. A temporary password will be sent automatically.

2

Sign In & Select Program

Log in using your email and temporary password. Browse undergraduate or postgraduate programmes and choose your preferred study scheme.

3

Upload Academic Documents

Attach scanned copies of your UCE / UACE Pass Slips, Degree/Diploma certificates, and National ID or Passport.

4

Pay Application Fee & Submit

Generate a Payment Reference Number (PRN) for UGX 50,000 (East Africa) or USD 50 (International). Pay via Mobile Money (MTN/Airtel) or designated bank.
